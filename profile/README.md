### What is HBVNK?

HBVNK is a 100% self-custodial financial wallet built on [Hyperliquid](https://hyperliquid.xyz). It brings confidential spot and perpetual trading and onchain yield strategies together in one app. Your keys are created and encrypted on your device and are never sent to HBVNK.

### Features

- **Wallet** — One self-custodial wallet across HyperCore and HyperEVM. Create or import a wallet, or sign in with email or a social account.
- **Trade** — Spot and perpetual markets on Hyperliquid, from crypto to gold and stocks, 24/7. Orders route through [HyperLink](https://docs.hyperlink.xyz), an encrypted prime broker, for confidential execution and lower fees.
- **Earn** — Automated, market-neutral yield on your stablecoins through [Liminal](https://docs.liminal.money).
- **HYPE rewards** — HBVNK uses 100% of its fees to buy and stake HYPE, and allocates 60–90% of them to user rewards.
